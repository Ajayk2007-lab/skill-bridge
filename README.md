# 🚀 SkillBridge – From Skills to Opportunities

SkillBridge is an **AI-powered career intelligence platform** that transforms resumes, projects, and professional profiles into a **living Skill Intelligence Profile**.  
It bridges the gap between **students, institutions, and industry** by mapping skills, detecting gaps, and matching opportunities with precision.

---

## 🌟 Vision & Mission
- 🎯 **Vision:** To create a unified ecosystem where skills meet opportunities seamlessly.  
- 🏆 **Mission:** Empower students, institutions, and recruiters with AI-driven insights that reduce hiring delays, improve industry readiness, and personalize learning paths.  

---

## 📊 Achievements & Impact
- ✅ **100K+ Skill Assessments** completed  
- ✅ **50K+ Opportunities Matched**  
- ✅ **99% Gap Detection Accuracy**  
- ✅ **98% Industry Readiness**  

---

## 🧩 The Problem We Solve
- **Academic Disconnect** → Curricula often fail to align with industry skill demands.  
- **Skills Gap Analysis** → Students struggle to identify competencies required for entry-level roles.  
- **Opportunity Misalignment** → Generic portals lack detailed skill intelligence for perfect fit.  
- **Industry Readiness** → Recruiters face difficulty in finding candidates with precise technical readiness.  

---

## ✨ Core Capabilities
- 🔹 **Skill Mapping** → Compare technical & soft skills against industry-specific role requirements.  
- 🔹 **Skill Gap Analysis** → Identify deficiencies and receive personalized learning paths.  
- 🔹 **Opportunity Matching** → Connect with internships and placements aligned to your unique profile.  

---

## 🔄 Unified Workflow
SkillBridge creates a **continuous ecosystem** connecting education to industry readiness:

**Student Side**  
- Student Profile  
- Skills Mapping  
- Upskilling  
- Industry Feedback  

**AI Side**  
- AI Skill Intelligence  
- Gap Analysis  
- Opportunities
